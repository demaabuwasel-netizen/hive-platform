# 🐝 Hive

Hive is an AI-powered platform that connects university students with NGOs through semantic matching instead of simple keyword filtering.

The platform helps students gain meaningful real-world experience while helping nonprofits discover motivated students whose skills, values, languages, and goals align with their mission.

---

# 🌍 Problem

Many students want to contribute to meaningful projects but struggle to find opportunities that truly fit their background and interests.

At the same time, NGOs often lack the resources and technical knowledge to recruit students effectively.

Current platforms rely mostly on keywords, forms, and traditional job-board logic.

Hive introduces a more human-centered matching experience.

---

# 💡 Solution

Hive uses semantic profile matching to connect students and NGOs based on:

- Skills
- Languages
- Goals and motivations
- Academic background
- Social causes and interests
- Mission compatibility

Instead of searching by keywords alone, Hive attempts to understand the meaning behind student profiles and NGO needs.

---

# ✨ Main Features

## Student onboarding
Students create profiles including:
- university
- field of study
- skills
- languages
- goals
- previous experience

---

## NGO dashboard
NGOs can:
- create opportunities
- manage applicants
- view AI-powered matches
- add multiple open roles

---

## Semantic matching system
The platform recommends opportunities based on compatibility between:
- skills
- languages
- interests
- NGO mission
- experience level

---

## AI explanation system
Each match includes an explanation describing:
- why the student fits the NGO
- which skills aligned
- how the mission matches the student profile

---

## Messaging flow
Students and NGOs can initiate communication through AI-assisted message suggestions.
